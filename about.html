About me

“Is that coffee?” “Nope, it’s actually Coke.”

I love the burst of bubbles on my tongue with my first sip. Graphic design gives me that same jolt, only through my eyes.

I was the girl drinking iced Coke in a coffee world. When I moved to the U.S. from China, language felt like an insurmountable wall. I feared being the “wrong person” in the “right place.” My iced Coke became a quiet comfort and a small rebellion, proof that it was okay to stand out.

Graphic design became my safe haven and my primary way of communicating. Where words fell short, visual hierarchy, color, and texture spoke clearly. I work where tactile materiality meets strategic visual communication. I want people to feel my work: the texture under their fingers, the spark of an unexpected color.

These days, I drink coffee too. I haven’t given up Coke; I’ve just stopped believing I have to choose.
